# Lesson 01 - Engineer Identity

## Goal

Define who I want to become as an engineer, beyond job titles and technologies.

## Why this matters

If I do not know what kind of engineer I want to be, I will keep chasing random tools and trends. Identity gives direction.

## Questions

1. Why do I build software?
2. What kind of problems excite me the most?
3. What kind of engineer do I admire?
4. What values will I not compromise?
5. What should clients and teammates remember me for?
6. What does success look like in 5 to 10 years?

# Lesson 02 - Learning System

## Goal

Build a learning routine that can survive busy weeks, low motivation, and distractions.

## Why this matters

I do not need a perfect week.

I need a repeatable week.

## Questions

1. When do I learn best?
2. What usually breaks my focus?
3. What learning methods work best for me?
4. How much time can I realistically commit every week?
5. What should happen when I miss a session?
6. What does a successful week look like?

# Lesson 03 - Baseline Assessment

## Goal

Measure where I am now so I can see real growth later.

## Why this matters

Without a baseline, I cannot tell whether I am improving or just feeling busy.

## Questions

1. What are my strongest technical skills today?
2. What are my weakest technical skills today?
3. What are my biggest career risks?
4. What kind of company environment helps me grow?
5. What kind of company environment makes me stagnate?
6. What is my current target role?
